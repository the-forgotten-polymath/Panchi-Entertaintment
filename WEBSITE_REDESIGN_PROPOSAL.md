## 4. Key Upgrades in the Redesign

### A. Ultra-Luxury Aesthetic (Visual Identity)
- **Palette & Typography:** Royal Gold accents (`#D4AF37`), Deep Onyx/Charcoal background (`#121212`), paired with editorial serif typography (*Playfair Display*) and clean modern sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Cinematic Experience:** High-performance background video reels, smooth scroll micro-animations, glassmorphism cards, and interactive hover states.

### B. Technical SEO & Google Search Readiness
- **Full Removal of Googlebot Restrictions:** Rewrite `robots.txt` and generate valid `sitemap.xml` for all 33 pages.
- **On-Page Meta Architecture:** OpenGraph tags (for WhatsApp/Instagram preview cards), schema markup for Event Planner & Artist Agency, and descriptive H1-H3 hierarchy.

### C. Direct WhatsApp Lead Funnels
- Each of the 13 ceremony pages and 8 artist pages will have a **one-touch WhatsApp action button** with pre-filled message prompts:
  > *"Hi Panchi Entertainment, I would like to check availability & quote for [Artist Name / Event Name] on [Date]."*
- Instant increase in conversion rate from mobile traffic.

### D. Extreme Speed & Asset Optimization
- Compression and WebP/AVIF format conversion for all 248+ images and 36 video streams.
- Deployment on global Edge CDN (Vercel / Cloudflare) ensuring **sub-2-second load times** even on mobile networks.
