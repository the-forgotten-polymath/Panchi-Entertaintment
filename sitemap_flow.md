## 2. Navigational Flow & User Journeys

### Flow A: The Wedding Family Journey (High Ticket)
1. **Discovery:** User lands on `index.html` → Captivated by hero showreel video.
2. **Exploration:** Clicks **Wedding Events** dropdown → Navigates to `/wedding-events.html`.
3. **Specific Ceremony Deep Dive:** Views ceremony specific pages (e.g. `/wedding-events/sangeet.html` or `/wedding-events/haldi.html`).
4. **Conversion:** Clicks **"Book Consultation on WhatsApp"** CTA → Direct WhatsApp routing with pre-filled ceremony text.

### Flow B: Celebrity Artist Booking Journey
1. **Discovery:** User searches for Diljit Dosanjh or Karan Aujla wedding booking → Lands on `/artists.html` or `/artists/diljit-dosanjh.html`.
2. **Credibility Building:** Watches performance highlights, reviews rider specs, sound & stage requirements.
3. **Action:** Clicks **"Check Artist Availability"** → Instant routing to Panchi Entertainment celebrity management desk on WhatsApp.

### Flow C: Corporate & Hospitality Funnel
1. **Discovery:** Corporate brand manager lands on `/corporate-events.html` or `/hospitality.html`.
2. **Evaluation:** Reviews VIP airport transfers, RSVP coordination, conference sound/AV.
3. **Action:** Submits lead capture form or calls direct hotline `+91 7895040431`.
