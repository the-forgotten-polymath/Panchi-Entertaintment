Royal Wedding & Event Hospitality

360-Degree Guest Care

Thoughtful Service from Arrival to Farewell

Eliminate guest confusion, delays, and stress with our synchronized concierge network.

Airport & Transit Logistics

Dedicated greeting desks at airport terminals and railway stations. Real-time flight tracking, personalized name placards, bottled spring water, and luxury chauffeur transfers directly to the venue.

Royal Traditional Welcome

Silver thali aarti, fragrant marigold or rose petal showers, shehnai or nagada welcoming music, cooling scented towels, and bespoke mocktails as guests step onto the celebration grounds.

Rapid Check-in & Hampers

Pre-coded electronic room keys, private dedicated check-in desk, luggage tagging and delivery to rooms within 15 minutes, with wedding itineraries and gift hampers thoughtfully laid out.

Bride & Groom Shadow Team

Personal shadows who remain unobtrusively by the couple and immediate parents — ensuring snacks, hydration, makeup touch-up coordination, wardrobe emergency kits, and timing alignment.

24/7 On-Site Guest Helpdesk

A physical branded helpdesk in the hotel lobby catering to express steam-pressing, hair/makeup stylist direction, doctor-on-call, courier assistance, and function transport coordination.

Bespoke Guest Itinerary App & Kits

Digital WhatsApp RSVP updates, custom printed event guidebooks with dress code suggestions, ceremony timings, key family contact numbers, and souvenir gift tags.
