Signature Special Effects & Pyrotechnics

Designed for breathtaking grand entries, electrifying Sangeet drops, romantic Varmala rituals, and showstopping concert finales.

Cold Pyro Sparklers

100% smokeless, odorless, non-flammable cold titanium sparks reaching 1 to 5 meters. Fully safe for indoor ballrooms, stage perimeters, runway paths, and couple entry moments.

Stadium 3D Laser Mapping

Multi-watt RGB laser projectors with high-speed scanners creating aerial beam tunnels, 3D geometric aerial shapes, and custom monogram beam projections synced to live music.

CO2 Cryo Jets & Confetti

Instantaneous 8-meter high cryogenic white smoke plumes that disappear in seconds with zero residue, paired with high-power continuous stadium metallic confetti blasters.

Dancing On The Clouds

Pure sub-zero food-grade dry ice heavy smoke that hugs the floor at ankle height without rising or blocking view—creating an ethereal heavenly floor for the couple's first dance and Varmala.

Synchronized Aerial Fireworks

High-altitude bespoke sky displays, multi-shot gold willow waterfalls, shimmering peony bouquets, and musical pyrotechnic choreographies lighting up the night sky for grand wedding finales.

Color Flame Projectors

DMX programmable color liquid flame machines projecting controlled bursts of vivid fiery accents in gold, green, red, and blue for high-voltage artist entries and rock concert stages.
